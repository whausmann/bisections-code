\begin{observation}\label{obs:search for sufficient bisection}
    Let $G$ be a cubic graph with girth at least five and a bridge,
    and suppose $|G|\leq 36$. Then $G$ has an external bisection
    $(L,R)$ such that $|L_3|=|R_3|\geq 4$.
\end{observation}

Observation~\ref{obs:search for sufficient bisection} was verified by
exhaustive computation using the code and recorded results
in~\cite{hausmann-bisection-search}.
The search generated all connected cubic graphs of girth at least five
with a bridge and at most $36$ vertices by joining pairs of rooted
bridge components, and checked each candidate for a sufficient external
bisection. All $149{,}585{,}205$ candidate constructions passed; this
count may include isomorphic copies.
